## In practice

As more of our social and practical life gets a digital extension, it becomes harder to choose to stand outside. Every individual should still be able to make that choice. That means being open about what joining means, telling people when they are included, and making it as easy to leave as it was to join.

## Questions to ask in your project

- Does everyone who is part of this service know that they are?
- Can people get what they need without using the digital service at all?
- How easy is it to leave, and what happens to their data when they do?
