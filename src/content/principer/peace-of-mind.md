## In practice

It is easy to think your product is more important than all others. But each person juggles many services every day, and our ability to concentrate hasn't changed in hundreds of thousands of years, while the competition for our attention keeps growing. Sometimes the greatest value of a service is that it frees people to focus on something more important.

## Questions to ask in your project

- How much of people's attention does our product really need?
- Does every notification serve the user, or our engagement numbers?
- Can people get what they came for quickly, and then leave?
