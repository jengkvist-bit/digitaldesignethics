## In practice

User involvement is the foundation of UX design, but this principle is also about the bigger picture. The digital layer of society deserves the same public attention as urban planning: how the city we live in is shaped decides whether we feel at home there. Giving people influence leads to services that make a real difference in their lives.

## Questions to ask in your project

- Which users have we actually talked to, and who is missing?
- Can users affect how the service develops after launch?
- What would our users change if they had the power to?
