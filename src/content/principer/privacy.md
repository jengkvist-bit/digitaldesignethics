## In practice

Laws like the EU's GDPR show how little regard many digital products have had for people's privacy, and how valuable user data is. Because that value is so high, there is every reason to be vigilant. If the main business of a product is to collect data about its users, think twice about designing it at all.

## Questions to ask in your project

- Is every piece of data we collect really necessary?
- Does the data benefit the users, or mainly us?
- Would our users be surprised if they saw what we know about them?
