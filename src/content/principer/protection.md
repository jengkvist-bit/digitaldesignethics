## In practice

Interaction between people is delicate, and a digital product shapes how well we can look out for each other. Cyberbullying is an example: online, bystanders often can't react or step in the way they would in the physical world. Design for the social contracts that keep people safe, not only for the individual user.

## Questions to ask in your project

- Can users report, block or react to behaviour that is not socially accepted?
- Can others step in and support someone who is being treated badly?
- What happens to a user who is targeted, and who is responsible for helping them?
