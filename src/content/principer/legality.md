## In practice

It is easy to be naive about how what you design can be abused. A small personality quiz app, where users answered questions to get a psychological profile, ended up supplying data for targeted campaigns in a presidential election, and led to fines in the billions. The people who designed it hardly had that in mind.

## Questions to ask in your project

- What is the worst illegal use of this product we can imagine?
- Could the data we collect be used for something our users never agreed to?
- If something illegal happens through the product, can it be noticed, and who is responsible?
