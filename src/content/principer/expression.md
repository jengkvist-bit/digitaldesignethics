## In practice

Freedom of expression is fundamental in the analogue world and should apply digitally too. Online it raises hard questions. Should people be able to say anything anonymously? Should large platforms be allowed to suspend people? Do we need stricter rules when one message can reach millions? The principle still stands: people should be free to express themselves.

## Questions to ask in your project

- Could this product silence people, or make them afraid to speak?
- Who decides what may be said here, and is that visible to users?
- How do we balance free expression with the protection in principle #2?
