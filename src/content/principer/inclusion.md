## In practice

Inclusion is about inviting as many people as possible to take part. It is closely tied to principle #4, Choice: if a service can't be used by people with a disability, they have no choice at all. That is why accessibility belongs in the process from the start, not as a fix at the end.

## Questions to ask in your project

- Who can't use this service today, and why?
- Is accessibility part of our requirements and tests, or an afterthought?
- Have we involved people with different abilities in the design?
