## In practice

Information is hardly scarce today. The challenge is to find your way in it. Access to neutral information is essential for a functioning democracy. How many of a search engine's results can be paid ads before it loses its neutrality? What information is reasonable to charge for, and what should be free for everyone?

## Questions to ask in your project

- Is it clear to users what is paid, promoted or filtered?
- What information do we hide from users, and why?
- Does the product help people learn and see other perspectives, or narrow their view?
