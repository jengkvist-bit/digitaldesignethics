## In practice

Once a product is out there, it may be too late to stop it from being misused. That is why this principle starts before the first sketch: know what you believe is morally right or wrong before you design anything, and think through how your product could be turned against the people it is meant to serve.

## Questions to ask in your project

- How could this product be used to watch, control or restrict someone?
- Who could be harmed if the product ends up in the wrong hands?
- What do we, as a team, consider unacceptable for this product to do?
