# We made this

<p class="ingress">We are One Day Interact. We made this tool because we are convinced that sustainability should be an important part of all design.</p>

By design, we seek to contribute to a positive development in a larger sense - for the individual, for humanity, the environment and the planet.

We made this to be freely used in the design community, there is no hidden business agenda.
