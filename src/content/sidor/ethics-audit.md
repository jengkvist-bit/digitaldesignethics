# Ethics audit

Ten principles. Five layers of analysis. One question: does this service actually work for the people it's supposed to serve? Describe a service or product concept below to find out.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>What kind of services can I audit?</summary>
<p>Anything digital — apps, websites, platforms, internal tools, public services. It works best for services with some complexity: something that involves data, decisions, organisations, or people with limited power or choice.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How is this different from a usability review?</summary>
<p>This is not about whether something is easy to use. It's about whether it's fair, honest, and respectful of the people it's supposed to serve. A service can be perfectly usable and still be ethically problematic.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is my input stored or used for training?</summary>
<p>This tool does not store or log your conversations. Queries are sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses. For information on how Anthropic handles API data, see their privacy policy at <a href="http://anthropic.com/privacy">anthropic.com/privacy</a>.</p>
</details>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I trust this audit?</summary>

</details>
